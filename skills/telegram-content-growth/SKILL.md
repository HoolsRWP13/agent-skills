# Telegram Content and Growth

## Process
1. Establish niche, audience segments, language, positioning, value proposition, competitor set, and owner goals.
2. Research only public information and cite sources for factual or time-sensitive claims.
3. Create a content strategy with repeatable pillars, formats, cadence, voice, calls to action, and moderation boundaries.
4. Produce a calendar containing date/time, objective, audience, format, draft, media brief, CTA, source notes, and approval state.
5. Draft posts with clear hooks, useful substance, accurate claims, accessible formatting, and no fabricated urgency or testimonials.
6. Repurpose owner-approved material into short posts, polls, FAQ, video scripts, and bot flows while preserving meaning and rights.
7. Propose growth experiments: collaborations, cross-promotion, public directories, referral mechanics that comply with platform rules, and useful lead magnets.
8. Define one primary metric per experiment, baseline, duration, sample limitations, and stop condition.
9. Review results and recommend changes based on observed data, not vanity metrics alone.

## Guardrails
- No unsolicited bulk DMs, purchased followers, fake views/reactions, engagement pods, impersonation, or misleading giveaways.
- Respect copyright, image rights, privacy, and advertising disclosure requirements.
- Never claim a draft was published or an experiment succeeded without evidence.
- Require owner approval for public campaigns, paid promotion, sponsorship commitments, or material changes in channel positioning.

## Deliverables
Positioning brief, editorial pillars, 2-4 week content calendar, ready-to-review drafts, media prompts/briefs, growth experiment backlog, and a concise performance review.
